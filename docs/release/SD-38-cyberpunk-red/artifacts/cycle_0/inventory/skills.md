## 2. Skill check formula and DV table (printed text)

**Skill Base** (p082, p129): "each Skill is also linked to a STAT ... you'll add the STAT that is linked to that particular Skill to get a total aptitude ... also known as a Skill Base." p129: "That's why you add STAT + Skill together on a Skill Check. This creates your Skill Base." p131: "A Skill Base is the appropriate STAT + Skill combined as a single number."

**Check, two forms** (p130):
- Opposed: "Attacker's STAT + Skill + 1d10 vs. Defender's STAT + Skill + 1d10." "In case of a tie, the Defender always wins."
- Against Difficulty Value: "Attacker's STAT + Skill + 1d10 vs. Difficulty Value (DV)." The check must beat the DV (example: roll 14 beats DV13).

**DV table** (p130, printed order):

| Difficulty | Description (abridged) | DV |
|---|---|---|
| Simple | "something most people can do without thinking, but which might be hard for a small child" | 9 |
| Everyday | "something most people can do without a lot of special training" | 13 |
| Difficult | "difficult to accomplish without training or natural talent" | 15 |
| Professional | "takes actual training and the user can be considered to be a professional" | 17 |
| Heroic | "highly skilled feat; one that only the best of the best can pull off" | 21 |
| Incredible | "a tremendous feat ... among the very best of your class professionally" | 24 |
| Legendary | "an awe-inspiring feat" | 29 |

(7 rows; `grep -cE "^ *(Simple|Everyday|Difficult|Professional|Heroic|Incredible|Legendary) " p130.txt` should return 7 or more; description lines are split across the layout, so the value pairing above is read from the page, not machine-parsed.)

**Dice rules** (p131):
- Critical Success: "When you roll a natural 10 on your d10 ... Roll another 1d10 and add the result to your first roll. If you roll another 10, you do not score another Critical Success."
- Critical Failure: "natural roll of 1 ... Roll another 1d10 and subtract the result from your STAT + Skill + the first roll. If you roll another 1, you do not score another Critical Failure." So 10s and 1s explode once only.
- Complementary Skills: at GM discretion, a good roll in one Skill "may confer a +1 bonus to the subsequent use of a related Skill ... affects a subsequent attempt once, and Complementary Skill bonuses do not stack."
- Taking Extra Time: "a single +1 bonus ... for taking four times longer."
- No Skill: "use the STAT that the Skill you don't have is linked to and add it to 1d10." (STAT + 1d10, no skill level.)
- LUCK: before the roll, spend LUCK Pool points, "+1 for each point ... expended." Pool equals LUCK STAT, refills each session.
- Trying Again: "If you fail a Skill Check, you can't try again unless your chances of success have improved."
- Modifiers: "subtract the Modifier Value from your roll", cumulative. Negative examples (p131): Night or low lighting -1; Never done this before -1; Complex task -2; No right tools or parts -2; Slept uncomfortable -2; Under extreme stress -2; Exhausted -4; Extremely drunk or sedated -4; Trying to perform task secretly -4; Task obscured by smoke, darkness -4. (10 rows.) Positive modifiers "generally come from gear, cyberware, Role Abilities, or drugs".
- Use a Skill action (p129): quick task, 3 seconds max.
- Repair sidebar (p141): Vehicle Repair DV by damage: Minor DV9 3 Hours; Major DV13 1 Day; Destroyed DV17 1 Week. Item Repair DV by Price Category: Cheap/Everyday DV9 1 Hour; Costly DV13 6 Hours; Premium DV17 1 Day; Expensive DV21 1 Week; Very Expensive DV24 2 Weeks; Luxury DV29 1 Month; Super Luxury "DV29, 1 Month per 10,000eb of Item Price". Failed roll: restart. The pages say "see sidebar on Page 140" (printed 140 = file p141). UNCLEAR: Luxury and Super Luxury both read DV29 as printed; pdftotext may be faithful to a printed oddity. Check the PDF image before encoding.
- Armor Penalty (p097, not a Skills page): armor "will lower your REF, DEX, and MOVE by the most punishing Armor Penalty ... only once ... minimum MOVE 0". Not mentioned in the Skills chapters; belongs to the armor inventory.

## 5. Text-extraction quality

- p083, p084, p085 and p086 are two-column. Rows read cleanly but interleave across columns (a left heading and right heading on one line). A line-oriented parser will mis-pair descriptions with headings. Per-name grep works.
- p083 and p133 have the "Stealth" heading in the Body block right-column; reading by column order puts it correctly after Resist Torture/Drugs.
- p132-p143 are two-column with sidebars. Garbled spots: p141 (Vehicle Repair and Item Repair sidebar is a narrow column interleaved with Air Vehicle Tech and Basic Tech text, so the DV list line breaks are scrambled); p129 and p131 have margin notes mixed into body text; p130 DV table splits each description across lines with the DV value on the wrong line.
- p143 carries the final four skills (Pick Lock, Pick Pocket, Sea Vehicle Tech, Weaponstech); Photography/Film runs from p142 onto p143.
- Minor typos in source: "prologed activity.." (p133), "their a language" (p086).
- Dotted-leader grep recall is 59 of 66 per list because some headings have a space before the dots or share a line with another heading. Do not use a generic regex to count skills.

## 6. Print vs compute split (standing rule: only values that feed sheet totals are computed; text rules are printed)

Compute (feeds a number on the sheet):
- Skill Base per skill = linked STAT + Skill level (ex. 4 + 6 = 10). STAT linkage from the table above (66 rows).
- Skill level entry: 1..10 range, Basic Skills floor 2, creation cap 6, (x2) doubles point cost. Point-spend totals (86) are creation bookkeeping; compute only if the sheet shows a points-spent figure.
- Language and Local Expert start values (Language (Streetslang) 2, cultural language 4, Local Expert (Your Home) 2).
- Evasion applies to ranged attacks only at REF 8 or higher (p084): print as text; do not compute.

Print (rule text only, no sheet total):
- Opposed check and DV check formula, DV table (7 rows), tie goes to Defender.
- Critical success/failure, Complementary +1, extra time +1, LUCK spending, no-skill rule, trying again, 10 modifier examples.
- Repair DV and time sidebar; specialty prompts (Science fields, instruments, Martial Arts forms).
- Armor Penalty to REF/DEX/MOVE: this affects Skill Base for REF/DEX skills, but it is an armor rule on p097. It is a candidate for compute in the armor inventory, not here.
- Base 10/14/18 flavor text per skill: print or omit. It carries no sheet-total value.
