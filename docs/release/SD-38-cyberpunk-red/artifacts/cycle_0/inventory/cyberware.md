## 2. Humanity / EMP rules (exact text)

Humanity = EMP x 10. p081: "For every point of Empathy the Character has, they gain 10 points of Humanity (HUM). For example, a Character who starts with 5 EMP would have 50 Humanity." Table on p081: EMP 1..10 maps to HUM 10..100. p230: "Empathy is a STAT, and for each point of EMP you have, you have 10 points of Humanity."

Loss lowers EMP by tens place. p081: "This happens every time the tens place of your Humanity value is lowered. For instance, a Character with 44 Humanity has an EMP of 4 until their Humanity is lowered to 39, at which point their EMP lowers to 3." p230: "Every time you lose enough Humanity that the ten's place on your Humanity changes, like going from 50 Humanity to 46 Humanity, your EMP is effectively lowered to the ten's place of your Humanity, in this case, EMP 5 to EMP 4." Note: p230's own example (50 to 46, EMP 5 to 4) agrees with p081 (44 stays EMP 4, 39 is EMP 3). So EMP = floor(current Humanity / 10). The two examples are internally consistent.

Loss on install. p112: "At Character Generation, Humanity Loss is preset. After Character Generation, Humanity Loss is determined by the dice in parentheses following the preset number." Install surgery is included in price (p111).

Cyberpsychosis threshold. p081: "If your Character's HUM drops below zero, that represents the death of your Character's emotional life; they slide into a state called cyberpsychosis". p233 "The Rules of Cyberpsychosis" by current EMP:
- "3 or Greater: No cyberpsychosis or dissociative disorder."
- "Equal to 2: Borderline dissociative disorder. Do your best to role-play accordingly."
- "Equal to 1: Dissociative disorder, borderline cyberpsychosis, Character exhibits strongly at least 3 traits from the Hare Psychopathy checklist."
- "Equal to 0: Cyberpsychosis. Character exhibits strongly at least 5 traits from the Hare Psychopathy checklist."
- "Equal to 0 and You Have Negative Humanity: Extreme cyberpsychosis. Character is handed to the GM, who plays them according to their worst tendencies. Character is handed back to the Player if their Humanity becomes a positive number again."
UNCLEAR: p081 says HUM "below zero" triggers cyberpsychosis while p233 begins the thresholds at EMP 2/1/0 (HUM 20-29, 10-19, 0-9). These are role-play bands, not the same trigger. UNCLEAR how EMP is derived for negative Humanity (floor of a negative number would be -1). p233 says EMP "Equal to 0" for the negative case, so clamp EMP at 0 (inference, not stated outright).

Non-cyberware Humanity loss (GM-applied), p232 table: Traumatic Physical Incident (torture) 1d6; Traumatic Mental Incident 1d6; Long-term Mental Stress 2d6; Long-term Environmental Stress 2d6. p110: "Humanity Loss of at least 6 points" for the scavvers example.

Therapy recovery (p231 table, p230 text):
- Standard Humanity Loss therapy: 500eb (Expensive), Medtech DV15, materials 100eb (Premium). "Patient regains 2d6 of their lost Humanity."
- Extreme Humanity Loss therapy: 1,000eb (V. Expensive), DV17, materials 500eb (Expensive). "Patient regains 4d6 of their lost Humanity."
- Both: "Humanity cannot be fully regained without the removal of cyberware. Each piece of cyberware will decrease your maximum Humanity by 2. Each piece of borgware cyberware lowers maximum Humanity by 4 instead. Cyberware with 0 Humanity Loss on installation will not decrease your maximum Humanity."
- Addiction therapy: 1,000eb, DV15, frees one addiction, no Humanity effect.
- p230: each therapy "takes 1 entire week"; "Medtechs can't do Therapy on themselves."
- Prices also at p377 (Night Market list).

UNCLEAR for the sheet: the max-Humanity penalty applies "per piece of cyberware". Options and foundations are all "pieces" by the table, but the text never says whether a free Standard Hand/Foot counts (it has no HL on the pre-installed copy). Proposed derivation: max Humanity = 10 x base EMP, minus 2 per installed non-borgware item with HL > 0, minus 4 per Borgware item. Needs operator confirmation before it is a computed field.

Upgrade Expertise (Tech role, p149): "Lower the Humanity Loss of non-borgware cyberware by 1d6 if its typical humanity loss would be 2d6 or greater." Also "Increase the number slots of a type an item already has for options ... by one." Both are GM-side events, not sheet-derivable.

Team Member NPCs (p156): "You do not need to lower their EMP due to Humanity Loss from cyberware. That's already been factored in."

## 4. Effects that must flow into sheet totals

## 7. Print vs compute split (standing rule)

Compute (feeds a sheet total):
- Humanity current, Humanity max, EMP from Humanity (tens place), and optionally the cyberpsychosis band label from the EMP.
- Sum of Humanity Loss presets for installed cyberware (needs the preset column from section 1).
- Body and Head SP from Skin Weave or Subdermal Armor, combined with worn armor by "highest SP wins".
- BODY from Grafted Muscle, Linear Frame Sigma, Linear Frame Beta, and the HP, Seriously Wounded, Death Save derived from it.
- Initiative modifier from Kerenzikov (+2). Sandevistan is conditional: print.
- Skill-level changes only if the sheet already shows skill totals: always-on skill bonuses (Amplified Hearing, Image Enhance, Voice Stress Analyzer, Toxin Binders, Medscanner, Techscanner, Light Tattoo and similar) are flat modifiers with a condition ("for Checks which include hearing"), so recommended handling is print-the-rule, not bake into the skill number. Skill Chip's "+3 floor" is a computed skill-level rule if skills are shown as levels.
- Option-slot usage vs capacity (7/7/7, NL 5, eye 3, audio 3, arm 4, leg 3) is computable but is a validation count, not a sheet-total input. Recommend the generator not enforce it.

Print (text only, no engine):
- All item descriptions, install tier, cost, price category, weapon stats for built-in weapons, pairing rules, the "does not stack with itself" notes, Sandevistan rules, Pain Editor, movement negations, Cyberdeck slot +1, Hardened Shielding, covering cosmetics, sexual implants, Cybersnake, Vampyres vial, tracer replacement price.

- GM-applied Humanity loss (trauma table p232, drug loss p228) is play-time, not sheet data.
