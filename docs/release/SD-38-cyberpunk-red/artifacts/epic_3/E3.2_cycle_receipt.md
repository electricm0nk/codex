## What landed

- **Gate:** `characterCreationGate('cyberpunk-red')` returns `{enabled: true, form: 'cyberpunk'}`; `CharacterHubPage`'s create mode renders `CyberpunkCreateForm` for it.
- **Form** (`CyberpunkCreateForm.tsx`): name; Role (options = `list_cyberpunk_catalog('role')` record names); method picker (Streetrat / Edgerunner / Complete Package buttons); STAT step per method — Streetrat: template-row select (options = the Role's `streetrat_template` records, labelled by the book line, or "Roll the row on 1d10"); Edgerunner and a rolled Streetrat row: "Roll again" (drops the seed) and the dice drawn; Complete Package: `-1/+1 <STAT>` per STAT and "Points spent: n of m" from the preview. STAT tiles (`cpr.stat.*`) and derived tiles (`cpr.derived.*`: Hit Points, Seriously Wounded, Death Save, Humanity, Current EMP) are the engine's rows from `preview_cyberpunk_character`; claim-blocking problems list and disable "Create character".
- **Save:** "Create character" sends `create_cyberpunk_character` with exactly the previewed STAT step, including the seed the preview drew (`withPreviewSeed`), so the saved STATs are the previewed ones; the command saves with `CprCharacterStore` and reads back through `load_saved_character_via_envelope` (→ `CyberpunkAdapter`, E3.1). The outcome shows the saved character's `cpr.stat.*` / `cpr.derived.*` rows; `onCreated` re-lists the Load list.
- **Layout registry** (`cyberpunkCreateLayout.ts`): `CYBERPUNK_CREATE_LAYOUT` of `LayoutBox<CyberpunkCreateBoxId, CyberpunkCreateColumn>` (`sheetLayout.ts`'s type and `boxesInColumn`): `identity`, `method` in `choices`; `stats`, `derived` in `statBlock`. The form renders its boxes from it.
- **No TSX re-derives a number:** the only arithmetic in the form is the `±1` on the player's own allocation (`adjustStat`, never below 0); the 2–8 range and the 62 total are the engine's refusals.
