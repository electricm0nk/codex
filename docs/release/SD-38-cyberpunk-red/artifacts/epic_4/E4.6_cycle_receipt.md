## What landed

`cpr_sheet_print::sheet_lines(catalog, sheet, choices, lifepath)` returns, in a fixed order:

1. **Box lines** (`kind` `cpr`): the composed sheet's printed lines in the engine's order, then the Lifepath answers (moved from the adapter, byte-identical output: the streetrat fixture did not change).
2. **`role_ability`** `cpr.print.role_ability.<role>`: label the ability, prose the `role_ability.json` text + page, `also` = the figure of every `cpr.role.<role>.*` row (the row detail before its first ": ", e.g. "Combat Awareness Rank 4", "Combat Awareness points 4", "NET Actions per Turn 3").
3. **`cyberware`** `cpr.print.cyberware.<i>`: per installed piece, prose its `cyberware.json` effect text + page, `also` "Humanity Loss <row cpr.cyberware.piece.<i>.humanity_loss>".
4. **`humanity`** `cpr.print.humanity_loss` (D-6, when any piece is installed): `also` "Humanity Loss <total row>", "Humanity <cpr.derived.humanity_current>", "EMP <cpr.derived.emp_current>"; prose "Humanity Loss L from installed cyberware: Humanity H = EMP E x 10 - L. EMP e: EMP is lowered by 1 every time the tens place of Humanity is lowered (p. 80-81)". Never "-2 EMP".
5. **`weapon_special`** `cpr.print.weapon.<i>`: a weapon with alternate fire modes (Autofire, Suppressive Fire, Shotgun Shell, Arrows, Explosive) or an Exotic Weapon (its own text); `also` "Skill Base <row>", "ROF <row>", and for Autofire "Autofire Skill Base <cpr.skill.autofire>".
6. **`netrunning`** (D-2): the engine's `cpr.netrunning.brain_damage` line re-kinded, `also` "Hit Points <cpr.derived.hit_points>", "armor SP does not apply". The D-2 revisit point ("change the printed line in `cpr_sheet_print.rs`") is this function.

Every number is read from a composed `cpr.` row; a record or row the line needs and the corpus/sheet lacks is refused by name. The sheet renders every line no box places under "Rules and features" (`RulesAndFeaturesSection`), so a printed line can never be dropped (the `Other totals` precedent for rows).
