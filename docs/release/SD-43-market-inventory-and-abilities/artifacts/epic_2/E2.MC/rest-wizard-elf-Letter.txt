Rest and Recovery

Natural healing
 With a full night's rest (8 hours of sleep or more), you recover 1 hit point per character level. Any significant interruption during your rest
 prevents you from healing that night.
 Core Rulebook, p.191
Healing ability damage
 Temporary ability damage returns at the rate of 1 point per night of rest (8 hours) for each affected ability score.
 Core Rulebook, p.191
Wizard: rest
 To prepare his daily spells, a wizard must first sleep for 8 hours.
 Core Rulebook, p.218
Wizard: preparation time
 After resting, a wizard must study his spellbook to prepare any spells that day. If he wants to prepare all his spells, the process takes 1
 hour.
 Core Rulebook, p.218
