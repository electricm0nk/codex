# SD-37 Starfinder seed hand values — spell save DCs (E4.4 supplement)

`seed-hand-values.md` (E0.4) carries spells per day (1st, 2nd) and spells known (0, 1st, 2nd)
for the two caster seeds, but no save DC. E4.4's criterion includes save DCs, so this file adds
one row per castable spell level, transcribed from the Starfinder Reference Document as hosted by
Archives of Nethys. Page sizes and sha256 digests are in `E4.4-srd-fetch-log.txt`. Same row shape
as E0.4 (`| SF-<seed> | <field> | <value> | <SRD URL + section> — <arithmetic> |`); the engine
fixtures read it through `seed_support::hand_values`.

**Rules applied:**

- Spell save DC = 10 + the level of the spell + your key ability score modifier
  (https://www.aonsrd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=106, Difficulty Class (DC): "For spells, this is 10 + the
  level of the spell + your key ability score modifier").
- Key ability: mystic Wisdom, technomancer Intelligence
  (https://www.aonsrd.com/Classes.aspx?ItemName=Mystic, Key Ability Score - Wis: "Your Wisdom
  determines … the saving throw DCs of your spells";
  https://www.aonsrd.com/Classes.aspx?ItemName=Technomancer, Key Ability Score - Int).
- Spell Focus: "The DC of spells you cast increases by 1. At 11th level … by 2, and at 17th level
  … by 3" (https://www.aonsrd.com/FeatDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Spell%20Focus, Benefit). Both caster
  seeds hold it (`seed-builds.md` §2 feats: Spell Focus at 3rd; §3: Spell Focus, technomancer
  bonus feat at 3rd, https://www.aonsrd.com/Classes.aspx?ItemName=Technomancer, Spell Focus -
  3rd Level). Both seeds are 5th level, so +1.
- Castable spell levels at 5th level: 0, 1st, 2nd (Spells Known table, 5th level: 6 / 4 / 3, no
  3rd-level column) for both classes.
- 0-level spells have no daily limit: "there is no limit to how many 0-level spells you can cast
  each day" (both class pages, Spells). No per-day number is printed for level 0.

**Inputs:** SF-Mystic-5 Wis 19 (modifier +4); SF-Technomancer-5 Int 19 (modifier +4)
(`seed-builds.md` §2/§3 `Final` rows; the same modifiers E0.4's reviewed rows use: SF-Mystic-5
Will "Wis mod +4", SF-Technomancer-5 Resolve "Int mod +4" in `seed-hand-values.md`).
