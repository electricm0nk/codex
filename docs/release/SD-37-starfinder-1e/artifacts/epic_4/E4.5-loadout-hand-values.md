# SD-37 Starfinder seed hand values: credits, bulk and bulk limits (E4.5 supplement)

`seed-builds.md` (E0.4, reviewed: "credits arithmetic and bulk totals" re-checked, §7) states
every seed's gear, each item's price and bulk with its SRD item page, and the credits and bulk
arithmetic. `seed-hand-values.md` carries no credits or bulk row. E4.5's criterion is "Credits
and bulk totals, encumbrance thresholds", so this file adds those rows in E0.4's row shape
(`| SF-<seed> | <field> | <value> | <SRD URL + section> — <arithmetic> |`). The engine fixtures
read it through `seed_support::hand_values`. Page sizes and sha256 digests are in
`E4.5-srd-fetch-log.txt`.

**Rules applied:**

- Starting wealth above 1st level: Table 11-5 Character Wealth per Level, 3rd = 4,000 credits,
  5th = 9,000 credits; the table "can also be used to budget gear for characters starting above
  1st level" (https://www.aonsrd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=230, Wealth By Level). 1st level = 1,000
  credits (https://www.aonsrd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=39, Step 8: Buy Equipment). This is E0.4's
  logged choice for every seed's gear budget (`seed-builds.md` §0, safe default).
- Item bulk: "a number, the letter 'L' if it has light bulk, or a dash ('—') if it has negligible
  bulk. … Every 10 items that have light bulk count as 1 bulk, and fractions don't count … Items
  that have negligible bulk count toward your bulk limit only if the GM determines that you are
  carrying an unreasonable number of them. Add together the numerical bulk values of all items you
  are wearing and carrying" (https://www.aonsrd.com/Equipment.aspx, Carrying Capacity: Item Bulk).
- Bulk limits: "You can carry an amount of bulk up to half your Strength score without
  difficulty. If you carry more than that, you gain the encumbered condition … You can't
  voluntarily wear or hold an amount of bulk that is greater than your Strength score. If you are
  forced to do so … you gain the overburdened condition" (https://www.aonsrd.com/Equipment.aspx,
  Bulk Limits). Bulk is a whole number, so "up to half" an odd score is the half rounded down.
- Encumbered: "reduce each of your movement speeds by 10 feet, reduce your maximum Dexterity bonus
  to AC to +2, and take a –5 penalty to Strength- and Dexterity-based checks". Overburdened:
  "reduce each of your movement speeds to 5 feet, reduce your maximum Dexterity bonus to AC to +0,
  and take a –5 penalty to Strength- and Dexterity-based checks" (same page, Encumbered,
  Overburdened). No seed is encumbered (`seed-builds.md` §5).

(Soldier), 10 (Mystic), 10 (Technomancer), 8 (Envoy).
