## What the rows do

Each SF seed is built end to end by clicks and selects only, then reopened:

1. `build-starfinder-<seed>`: Starfinder 1e → New Character → race, theme, class (+ key ability, the creation picks the form asks for: human +2 stat bonus → Str, Lashunta damaya subrace + Empath connection + the two student skills, Android scholar's Physical Science, Ysoki's improvisation Inspiring Boost) → the point-buy buttons (`seed-builds.md` §1–§4: 3/4/2/1, 2/7/1, 2/2/5/1, 1/2/7) → Create character → Back → Load → open the sheet → **Level up** to the seed's level with its skill ranks (the level-up owes the ranks of every level not yet taken, so level 2 adds two per skill; 15 / 40 / 40 / 27 ranks) and the 5th-level four-score increase where the seed has one → **Feats, spells and gear**: its feats, its spells known per castable level (the mystic's 15 including the Empath's two connection spells, the technomancer's 13), its gear equipped or carried → Save choices.
2. `open-starfinder-<seed>` (setup = the build row): the sheet's **Open** → Load Character list → the seed → Load; every figure is therefore read back from the saved character, not from the build's own screen. `expect` is the sheet's own `Label<TAB>value` text for every hand value the sheet prints (BAB, three saves, HP, Stamina, Resolve, EAC, KAC, starting credits / spent / remaining, bulk and both bulk limits, each trained skill, spells per day and known for levels 1 and 2), plus the character's name; `forbid` carries the Pathfinder sheet's `☰ Menu`, CMB, CMD and Flat-Footed.
3. `open-seed-aldric`, `open-seed-elowen`: Load Character → the starter seed (the app seeds both in a fresh data root) → Load; expects the name, `Fighter 3` / `Wizard 5` and the Pathfinder sheet's `☰ Menu`.
