## What is chosen, and where each answer comes from

| Choice | Offered / checked by | Saved as |
|---|---|---|
| feats | every `feat` record of the Starfinder package (224 principals: `python3` over `data/starfinder-1e/sheet_rules/*/*/*.json` counting `provenance.kind == "feat"` rows with no `#` in the id; the test pins the list to `rules_of_kind("feat")` principals); each one's prerequisite is its own `applies`, evaluated by the engine (`evaluate_applies`) over the character's held set with the chassis's BAB | `selected_feats` (theme and every other held pick kept) |
| a chosen feat's own pick (Weapon Focus's weapon type) | `sf_creation::discover` over the held set: a slot whose choosing rule is a chosen feat (optional) | rule choices `(feat, option)` |
| spells known | for each casting class and castable level: `sf_spells` known total and its terms; options = spell records whose `ClassSpellList` grant puts them at that level (`sf_sheet_print::spell_level_on`) | `spells_selected` (`Known`; other modes kept) |
| gear | every `equipment` record, labelled with its own `Price` row (printed); on the sheet an item is added through `append_to_character` → `StarfinderAdapter::append_to_character` (E4.6), at creation and level-up it joins the choices | `equipment_selections`: equipped (`EquippedActive`) or carried (`SelectedInactive`) |
| upgrades, fusions, materials | every `equipment_modifier` record that `sf_sheet_print::modifier_fits` the item | the selection's `applied_modifiers` |

Computed, never kept by hand: every total is `sf_adapter::compute_sheet` over the character with the choices applied; the preview returns its `sf.eac`, `sf.kac`, `sf.credits.*`, `sf.bulk` rows. Refused by name (blocking, nothing saved): a prerequisite the engine evaluates as unmet (`sf_choices.feat_prerequisite`), a non-repeatable feat twice (`sf_choices.feat_twice`), an unknown feat or item, a spell off the class's list (`sf_choices.spell_not_offered`), more spells than known (`sf_choices.spells_over_known`), a modifier that does not fit, and every `compute_sheet` refusal (`sf_loadout.over_budget`, two armours, upgrade slots). Printed, not counted (`decisions.md §5`): the advancement table's feat rule and every feat pool the held set carries (`feat_rules`).
