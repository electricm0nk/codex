## What the oracle says (and why the engine change)

The drone is a follower. The mechanic's AI selection `Drone` (`scr_abilities.lst:1369`) raises the master's `DroneCompanionLVL` and offers a follower of role `Drone` (`FOLLOWERS:Drone|1 COMPANIONLIST:Drone|Drone`). The companion modifier `FOLLOWER:DroneCompanionLVL=1 TYPE:Drone` (`scr_companionmods.lst:13`) gives that follower its special abilities, its chassis / skill unit / feat / mod picks, and its level variables **from the master**: `BONUS:VAR|DroneMasterLVL|MASTERVAR("DroneCompanionLVL")`, then `DroneLVL = DroneMasterLVL`. Every drone number (base attack `(DroneLVL+1)*3/4`, saves `DroneLVL/2+2` / `DroneLVL/3`, skill-unit ranks `DroneMasterLVL`, the feat and mod pick counts) reads that master variable. The engine evaluates `Expr::MasterVar` as unresolved words, always (`sheet_rule.rs`, SD-36 engine-P1-2: "until a real master-facts link exists"). Without a master link the drone block cannot resolve a single number, and its gate (`MasterVar >= 1`) prints "this character has no master". No print-path file can supply the link: `render_sheet` evaluates inside `sheet_rule.rs`. That file is E2's (`§3` E2 row). E2 is complete.
