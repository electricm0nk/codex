## Findings

1. **Three of the five Hit Point / Stamina terms are not in the SF package; E4.1 must source them elsewhere (routed to E4.1, not an E3 defect).** The converted package carries only the class coefficient lines (`core/class/soldier.json`: `#bonus1 Hp 6×L`, `#bonus2 Stamina 7×L`) and Toughness's `Stamina` line. These three terms have no converted record:
   - `HD:1` (one point per level). It is a class token, not a `BONUS:HP`, and no package line states it.
   - `RaceHP`. The value (`BONUS:VAR|RaceHP|4` for Lashunta) sits on `Lashunta Race Selection ~ Default` and the sum on `Default` (`BONUS:HP|CURRENTMAX|RaceHP`). Both are `CATEGORY:Internal` abilities in `scr_abilities.lst`, which are not E0.3 units. `grep -rln RaceHP data/starfinder-1e/sheet_rules` → no file.
   - `CON*TL`. It is on the `Constitution` stat definition, which is not a record.

   So M2–M4 are load-bearing only on E3.3's seed-fixture test, and that test models the terms from the table. E3.4's `E3.4_seed_terms.py` states the same thing: it "adds the terms the package does not hold as record lines". Summing the package's lines alone gives Soldier-3 HP 18, not 25. E3.3's criterion says the mutations must turn a seed fixture red, and the planted fixtures do go red, so E3's criteria hold. **E4.1** (generic chassis: "HP (race HP + class HP × level, per E3.3's mapping)") must read HD, race HP and Con × level from a source it names, and must re-plant M2–M4 against its own `sf_seed` test. E4's §3 row holds the converter (CONVERTER LANE), so it can also lower the race-selection `RaceHP` into the package. This is logged in `progress.md ## DISCOVERED` and in E4.1's `kanban.md` row note. It is not a new card, because E7.3 pins 55 rows (the E1.MC precedent).
2. **E3.5's "one shape" for the 10 non-additive core deltas is two shapes:** 6 `Fact → Rule` and 4 `Holds → AtLeast{n:1}` over several declaring rules. Both resolve the fact to the rules that declare it, and E3.5's `fact_gate_check` already counts all 10 as resolved. This sharpens E3.5's wording and does not correct its figure.
3. **`progress.md` Summary was stale:** it said E3 was 3 of 6 and the total was 18 of 55, when 21 cards were complete before this card. Recount: `awk -F'|' '$2 ~ /^ (C|E)[0-9]/ && $5 ~ /^ complete *$/' kanban.md | awk 'END{print NR}'` → 21, and a Python row parse also gives 21. This card corrects it to E3 6 of 6 and a total of 22.
