## Disposition — every one of the 2 live violations, false-positive or real, and which fix

| # | File:line | Text | Verdict | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `progress.md:131` | "...folded into a false 100%: 5,478 (`equipment` remainder + ..." | **false-positive** — "false 100%" here is the bundle-wide *name* of the anti-pattern (`decisions.md` §2 / `AGENTS.md` rule 2's "a `complete` claim over a slice, not the population"), coined and reused verbatim across `progress.md` and every Epic 5 receipt (13 occurrences repo-wide — see the Figures table's re-derive command). It states no measured completion rate over any population — there is no denominator to give it, because it isn't a figure subject to `decisions.md` §2 at all. The number after the colon (`5,478`) is an unrelated count of not-yet-examined units, not a denominator for that false 100%. | **matcher** |
| 2 | `progress.md:502` | "...would be the false-100% shape `decisions.md §2` and `AGENTS.md` rule 2 exist to prevent." | Same idiom, hyphenated form. Same reasoning. | **matcher** |

**No prose was reworded.** Both survivors are legitimate uses of the anti-pattern's own name; changing the prose would either break the cross-bundle idiom (13 occurrences use this exact phrase to point at `decisions.md` §2) or add a fake denominator to a sentence that isn't reporting a rate at all — itself a worse defect than the one being fixed.

**Proof this is not a rationalization for loosening the gate:** before writing the fix, I ran a
counterfactual across the full default scope — is there any line, anywhere in the checked files,
where the false-100% idiom sits on the same line as a *separate*, genuinely-unqualified percentage
that would be shadowed by exempting the idiom? (a small script diffing regex matches
before/after stripping the idiom text; see transcript in RED→GREEN evidence below.)
**Zero such lines exist in the current default scope.** The matcher change removes exactly the two
violations named above and nothing else — confirmed by the `files_checked=17 → violations=2 → 0`
transition being the *only* change in gate output.

## What landed — the matcher fix

`scripts/denominator_gate.py`: added a regex matching the exact false-100% idiom phrase (space or
hyphen form, case-insensitive). In `find_violations`, each line has that idiom text blanked out
before the percent scan runs — only the idiom's own matched text is removed; every other
percentage on the line (a real, separate claim) is still scanned in full and still requires its
own denominator on that same line. Nothing about the fenced-code-block exclusion, the
`of`/`out of`/`N/M`/`denominator` marker patterns, or the line-level granularity changed. (Exact
source: `scripts/denominator_gate.py`'s `FALSE_100_IDIOM_RE` definition and its use inside
`find_violations`.)
