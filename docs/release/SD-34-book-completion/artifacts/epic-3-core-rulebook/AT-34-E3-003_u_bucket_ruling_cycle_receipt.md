- **Discoveries:**
  1. **18 of `core_rulebook`'s 58 named `U` units — including the ruling's
     own worked examples `BANE`, `FLM_BRST`, `FRT_HVY` — do NOT match the
     conservative `!has_real_description` predicate**, because their
     corpus record DOES carry a real description
     (`corpus_json_has_real_description` finds it); they are blocked by
     the pre-existing `corpus_json_description_leaks_pcgen_syntax` guard
     instead, and fall through to the SAME pre-existing `unmeasurable`
     evidence string, indistinguishable by evidence alone from a
     genuinely-empty record. Re-deriving all 30 remaining
     `equipment_modifier` `unmeasurable` units' underlying corpus
     descriptions (a temporary diagnostic test, not committed — see
     `docs/retro/events/sd34-u-ruling-2.jsonl`) splits them into two named
     sub-causes:
     - **21 units genuinely carry an unresolved PCGen substitution**:
       `%CHOICE` (`BANE`, `BANE_AMF`, `BloodHunting`, `BloodHunting_AMF`,
       `SpiritHunting`, `SpiritHunting_AMF`, `Special Ability ~ Defiant ~
       Armor` — a real player choice, e.g. "which creature type", this
       engine does not model) or `%d<N>` (`FLM_BRST(_AMF)`,
       `ICY_BRST(_AMF)`, `SHK_BRST(_AMF)`, `THUNDER(_AMF)`, `PKIN_BR_{A,M,R}`,
       `Special Ability ~ Psychokinetic Burst ~ {Ammunition,Melee,Ranged}`
       — an unresolved critical-multiplier dice reference). These are
       closer to `decisions.md §17`'s own `X`-bucket discussion (a real
       chargen-time choice the backend would need to filter) than to the
       "nothing to compute, nothing to show" shape the `U`-ruling
       describes, and were **not** reclassified without a further ruling.
     - **9 units are blocked by a separate, confirmed `render_pcgen_desc`
       defect**, unrelated to `decisions.md §17`: `FRT_HVY`, `FRT_LGHT`,
       `FRT_MOD`, their 3 full-prose siblings (`Special Ability ~
       Fortification / {Heavy,Light,Moderate} ~ Armor`), the 2 `Special
       Ability ~ Ghost Touch ~ {Amulet of Mighty Fists,Weapon}` records,
       and `Special Ability ~ Deathless ~ Armor` all carry a CLEAN
       description with a real, digit-preceded percent sign quoting a
       chance fraction in prose (e.g. FRT_HVY's corpus description, "chance
       to negate critical hits…"). `leaked_pcgen_syntax`'s own bare-`%`
       check correctly exempts a `%` immediately preceded by a digit as a
       literal percent sign — but `render_pcgen_desc`'s DROP logic does
       not apply that same exemption, and strips the `%` anyway (the
       quoted fraction renders with the percent sign silently removed,
       `dropped_args: ["%"]`), which then trips `corpus_json_description_leaks_pcgen_syntax`'s
       `!rendered.dropped_args.is_empty()` check. This is a real, narrow
       renderer bug (confirmed directly against `render_pcgen_desc` and
       `leaked_pcgen_syntax`'s own source), **out of this cycle's scope**
       to fix (a `render_pcgen_desc` change needs its own corpus-wide
       re-verification, per `decisions.md §5`, since that function serves
       every book's equipment/spell/monster-ability descriptions, not only
       equipment modifiers).
     Logged as a `deferral` retro event
     (`docs/retro/events/sd34-u-ruling-2.jsonl`,
     `1787922788802-sd34-u-ruling-2-dc9418`).
