# Bestiary 6 Ledger -- SD-31 Wave 24

## The question the operator actually asked

**Is the remaining work a WIRING problem or an UNWIRED problem?**

Of Bestiary 6's 72 units, 32 are DONE (up from 26 at dispatch) and 40 are not. Of those 40:

| Classification | Count | Share of the 40 |
|---|---:|---:|
| WIRING GAP -- content present and connected, machinery cannot ground it | 39 | 97.5% |
| UNWIRED -- content not connected at all | 1 | 2.5% |
| NOT PRESENT -- oracle genuinely has no content | 0 | 0% |
| BLOCKED-RULING -- needs an operator decision before it can move | 0 | 0% |

**The answer is unambiguous: this is a WIRING problem, not an UNWIRED problem, by a 39:1 margin.**
Every one of Bestiary 6's remaining 16 `monster_ability` units and 18 `class_feature` units has
real, oracle-verified prose or a real formula behind it; none of the 40 units needed a Structural
Exclusion Register entry, and the wave-24 `monster_ability` lane's proposal to file 16 of them as
`NOT PRESENT` (licensing denominator removal) was independently reviewed GAMED and is corrected
here -- see `OPEN-ISSUES.md` row 351. The single `UNWIRED` unit (`Adopted Race ~ Rougarou`) is a
genuinely un-ingested selector row, not a missing-oracle-content case either.

**What one book at 72 units cost, so the remaining thirty can be priced:** this wave ran 4 lanes in
parallel plus one integration cycle. Of the 4 lane dispatches, 2 (`class_feature`, `race_trait`)
correctly identified their entire remaining population as out of their granted file scope and shipped
zero code -- a real, useful finding (a precise diagnosis of WHERE the blocker lives), not wasted
effort, but zero units banked. 1 lane (`monster_ability`) also shipped zero code and its diagnosis
was wrong (see above). 1 lane (`spell`) shipped real content that needed a same-cycle regression fix.
**The actual unit movement this wave (+6: 1 `race` + 5 `race_trait`) came from a SIXTH source: the
integration cycle itself, building the one fix (Rougarou's race ingest) that the dispatch had
explicitly barred every lane from touching** ("do NOT touch race chassis attribution"), once two
independent adversarial reviews established it was routine, already-precedented work rather than the
unbuilt heritage-mechanism the dispatching lane believed it to be.

**Priced honestly, that means: a lane dispatch bounded to "tables and matchers only, no chassis work"
is close to a guaranteed zero-yield dispatch for `race_trait` and a near-zero-yield one for
`class_feature`/`monster_ability` in their current shape.** The real levers for the next 30 books are
not more lane-parallelism at this file-scope grain; they are the SAME handful of subsystem widenings
this ledger names for every WIRING GAP row below -- a domain-power grounding subsystem widened past
Good+Healing, a monster_chassis <-> companion-ability bridge, and a race-trait flat-override compute
seam -- each of which, once built, closes units across MANY books simultaneously, not just Bestiary 6.
Bestiary 6 was small enough to fully diagnose in one wave; it was not small enough to fully CLOSE in
one wave, and the 6 units it did close came from lifting a scope restriction, not from working harder
inside it.

## Two rulings surfaced by this ledger

Both resolved by operator ruling 2026-08-21 (`OPERATOR-RULINGS-2026-08-21.md` §18/§19), folded into
`decisions.md` as Decision 18 and Decision 19 at wave 25b integration.

1. **CLOSED under Decision 18.** Cleric/Inquisitor Domain Power is a single, EXCLUSIVE,
   once-per-character choice -- NOT the same shape as Rogue Talent/Rage Power's genuinely open,
   repeatable-pick pools. The "browsable reference regardless of selection" pattern is **not** honest
   for an exclusive pool. The 3 units this row named (Intense Celebration, It Came From Beyond, The
   Stars Are Right -- Void/Scalykind/Dark Tapestry domains) may **not** bank via
   `REGISTERED_POOL_GROUPS`. They still need the same real domain-power grounding fix as the other 15
   -- the `domain_power` interpreter merged wave 25b (Good/Healing/War/Strength) is that fix's shape,
   but Void/Scalykind/Dark Tapestry are not yet in its catalog (their corpus records lack the
   `Domain<X>LVL`/`Domain<X>Times` chain the catalog depends on) and remain open work, unchanged by
   this ruling.
2. **CLOSED under Decision 19.** Bestiary 6's 2 spells are byte-identical reprints of spells Ultimate
   Wilderness already ships. Settled doctrine, not a new architectural question: under `decisions.md
   §13` branch 1 (identical printing, FIRST print owns it -- Ultimate Wilderness predates Bestiary 6),
   Ultimate Wilderness's copy is the one served to players (unchanged -- the wave-24 cross-book dedup
   pass stays exactly as it was), and Bestiary 6's own two units are reclassified from `not-ingested`/
   WIRING GAP to `text-complete` with real supersession evidence in the work inventory
   (`v06_work_inventory.rs`'s `classify()`, a fixed 2-item allowlist per the ruling's own "do not build
   a cross-book-reprint crediting mechanism"). **This closes the ledger question but does NOT move the
   mandate headline** -- both units' `wiring_class` is `derived`, and `derived`'s doneness lattice maps
   only `literal-verified`/`fixture-verified` to `DONE` (`text-complete` maps to `HELD`); forcing a
   `DONE`-mapped status would game `doneness_verdict()`, so the honest replayed result is `HELD`, not
   `DONE`. See `OPEN-ISSUES.md` row 358 for the surfaced ruling this leaves open. No general crediting
   mechanism was built.
